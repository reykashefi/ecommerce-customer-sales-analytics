# Business Questions

## E-Commerce Customer & Sales Analytics

This document defines the business questions and analytical objectives of the project.

The purpose of this analysis is to understand sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, customer value, and retention using SQL.

All analysis follows a consistent analytical definition of valid sales.

---

# 1. Analytical Sales Rule

A **Valid Sales Order** must satisfy all of the following conditions:

1. Order status is `Completed`
2. Order date is greater than or equal to the customer's signup date
3. The order contains at least one order item

### Revenue Definition

Revenue is calculated as:

`Revenue = Quantity × Price`

Only valid sales orders are included in official sales KPIs.

### Current Valid Sales Baseline

| KPI                |     Value |
| ------------------ | --------: |
| Valid Sales Orders |       656 |
| Valid Customers    |       262 |
| Valid Revenue      | 291331.00 |
| Valid Units        |      4536 |

These values are the baseline for subsequent analysis.

# 10. KPI Definitions

| KPI                  | Definition                                                                                         |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Valid Sales Orders   | Completed orders occurring on or after customer signup date and containing at least one order item |
| Valid Customers      | Customers with at least one valid sales order                                                      |
| Revenue              | Sum of Quantity × Price for valid sales                                                            |
| Units                | Sum of Quantity for valid sales                                                                    |
| AOV                  | Revenue divided by Valid Sales Orders                                                              |
| Revenue per Customer | Revenue divided by Valid Customers                                                                 |
| Repeat Customer      | Customer with at least two valid completed orders                                                  |
| Repeat Purchase Rate | Repeat Customers divided by Valid Customers                                                        |
| Recency              | Days since latest valid purchase                                                                   |
| Frequency            | Number of valid completed orders                                                                   |
| Monetary             | Total valid revenue generated by a customer                                                        |
| Revenue Share        | Revenue of a product, category, customer, or segment divided by total valid revenue                |

---

# 11. Analytical Principles

The following principles apply throughout the project:

1. Raw data must not be deleted.
2. Data-quality issues must be handled through analytical rules.
3. Revenue must be calculated as `Quantity × Price`.
4. Completed orders occurring before customer signup are excluded from valid sales analysis.
5. Orders without order items cannot generate line-item revenue.
6. `order_id + product_id` is not automatically treated as a unique key.
7. All major metrics must use consistent definitions across the project.
8. All analysis must be reproducible using SQL.
9. Business conclusions must be supported by calculated evidence.
10. Descriptive findings must be separated from business recommendations.
11. Invalid records should remain available for data-quality analysis.
12. Sales KPIs must use the Valid Sales Order definition.
